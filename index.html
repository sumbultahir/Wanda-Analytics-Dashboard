⚠ Top finding — the "English only" language gate

Wanda refuses to engage with non-English messages before attempting to interpret them — 48 of 388 conversations (~12%, roughly 1 in 8) hit this wall. Most affected users eventually got help after re-typing in English; some got zero assistance and the conversation simply ended. A second, distinct variant showed up 5 times: the block fired mid-conversation, on a later turn, even after Wanda had already responded normally in English earlier in the same thread. The Knowledge team flagged this independently too: users starting conversations in non-English languages was logged as a High-frequency observation.

Recommendation: at minimum, attempt to interpret the request before refusing; ideally, support responses in-language.

Client vs. Talent — who's asking, and how well is it going

Splitting the same 388 conversations by who's likely asking: a Client/HR Manager acting on behalf of their company (invoicing, admin permissions, hiring cost estimates, managing a talent's record) vs. a Talent/Employee handling their own PTO, payslip, or profile.

Methodology note: the export has no explicit user-role field, so this split is inferred from question phrasing (self-referential "my PTO / my payslip" → Talent; admin actions like "add a user," "approve expense," "invoice" → Client).
