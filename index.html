⚠ Top finding — the "English only" language gate

Wanda refuses to engage with non-English messages before attempting to interpret them — 48 of 388 conversations (~12%, roughly 1 in 8) hit this wall. Most affected users eventually got help after re-typing in English; some got zero assistance and the conversation simply ended. A second, distinct variant showed up 5 times: the block fired mid-conversation, on a later turn, even after Wanda had already responded normally in English earlier in the same thread — including one case where it fired twice in a row on the same message. For a platform serving a multinational workforce, this is the single most consistent failure pattern found.

Recommendation: at minimum, attempt to interpret the request before refusing; ideally, support responses in-language. Separately, investigate why the block sometimes fires mid-thread after Wanda has already engaged normally.
